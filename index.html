How the assistant decides

Simple logic for the beginner prototype

1. Transfer — one store has extra units while another has low stock and faster sales.

2. Reorder — stock is low and other stores cannot cover the demand.

3. Markdown — stock is old and sales are slow.

4. Wait — stock and demand look balanced.
